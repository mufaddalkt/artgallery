## Catalogue and interpretation sources

The gallery descriptions and stories are newly written summaries and visual observations, using the following museum sources. Museum affiliation is informational; the institutions have not endorsed this independent gallery.

- [Louvre catalogue: Mona Lisa](https://collections.louvre.fr/ark:/53355/cl010062370). Date used: 1503–1519, as in the current museum catalogue; oil on poplar.
- [MoMA: The Starry Night](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/79802). Saint-Rémy, June 1889; oil on canvas.
- [Mauritshuis: Girl with a Pearl Earring](https://www.mauritshuis.nl/en/our-collection/artworks/670-girl-with-a-pearl-earring). c. 1665; oil on canvas; tronie and pearl-painting details.
- [Rijksmuseum: Night Watch video tour](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/stories/one-hundred-masterpieces/story/night-watch-video-tour) and [10 things about the Night Watch](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/stories/10-things/story/10-things-about-rembrandt-s-night-watch). 1642; oil on canvas; guards' actions and 1715 trimming.
- [Uffizi: Birth of Venus](https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/birth-of-venus). c. 1485; tempera on canvas; arrival at Cyprus and qualified identifications of the figures.
- [Art Institute: A Sunday on La Grande Jatte — 1884](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/27992/a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte-1884), [museum API catalogue record](https://api.artic.edu/api/v1/artworks/27992), and [Seurat exhibition research](https://archive.artic.edu/seurat/seurat_themes.html). 1884–1886, border added 1888–1889; oil on canvas.
- [National Museum: The Scream, NG.M.00939](https://www.nasjonalmuseet.no/en/collection/object/NG.M.00939). 1893 version; museum medium “Tempera og fettstift på papplate” rendered as tempera and wax crayon on cardboard. Photography credit from the museum catalogue.
- [Art Institute: The Bedroom](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/28560/the-bedroom), [museum API catalogue record](https://api.artic.edu/api/v1/artworks/28560), and [Van Gogh's Bedrooms: about the paintings](https://archive.artic.edu/van-gogh-bedrooms/about-paintings). Chicago's second version, September 1889; oil on canvas.

Photographic colours, surface appearance and image aspect ratios reflect the particular source reproduction. The gallery uses local 1920-pixel maximum-dimension images and 420-pixel maximum-dimension thumbnails.
